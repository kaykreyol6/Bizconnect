📜 Applicable International Regulations
The following international commerce regulations govern all transactions on this platform. You are required to read and understand each one.
WTO
World Trade Organization guidelines apply to all cross-border commerce. Parties must comply with non-discrimination, transparency, and reciprocity principles.
CISG
UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods governs international procurement contracts. Formation, performance, and breach remedies are standardised.
GDPR
EU General Data Protection Regulation applies to all users whose data is processed. Right to access, erasure, and portability are guaranteed.
eIDAS
Electronic signatures on this platform are legally valid under EU eIDAS Regulation and equivalent national laws including ESIGN Act (USA).
ICC
Incoterms 2020 apply to all international shipping and delivery terms. Parties must specify applicable Incoterm in each contract.
LOCAL
Local regulations of both buyer and seller jurisdictions apply simultaneously. Platform automatically detects applicable local law based on registered address.
